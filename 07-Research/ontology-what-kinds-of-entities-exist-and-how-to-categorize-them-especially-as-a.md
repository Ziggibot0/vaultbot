## Connections to Vault Architecture

This research grounds the **ontology layer** of [[Knowledge-Triad-Ontology-Epistemology-Hermeneutics]]. The key mapping:

- The vault's entity types (directives, procedures, research notes, chat logs, textbook indexes) form an implicit taxonomy. The wikilinks between them are the relationships.
- A taxonomy organizes categories as hierarchy; an ontology defines semantic meanings and logical connections between entities [sources: Neo4j]. The vault currently has taxonomy (entity types) but only flat ontology (untyped wikilinks).
- [[Pre-Thought-Information-Shapes]] proposed typed edges as the mechanism to upgrade from taxonomy to ontology. The typed-edge vocabulary (provenance, structural, tension, support, causal, generative, lifecycle) IS an ontology definition.
- [[Vault-Thinks-LLM-Synthesizes]] chose to keep the ontology in prose rather than syntax — a narrative ontology. The formal ontology (typed edges) is deferred until the LLM can't synthesize without it.
- The ontology layer is the foundation: you must define what exists and how it relates before you can validate it (epistemology) or interpret it (hermeneutics). See [[Knowledge-Triad-Ontology-Epistemology-Hermeneutics]] for the full chain.
