## Key Findings
- To successfully explore deep learning techniques and improve the information extraction results, a dataset with more than twenty-five thousand documents has been compiled, anonymized and is published as a part of this work.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- We introduce DocArena-79K with QA pairs from 8,336 documents spanning 16 domains and 49 languages.  [sources: DocArena: Turning Raw Documents into Controllable Training Environments for Document Search Agents]
- In this workshop, we provide a better understanding of keyword and keyphrase extraction from the abstract of scientific publications.  [sources: Keyword Extraction in Scientific Documents]
- Neural networks have been successfully applied before - even though they have been trained only on relatively small datasets with hundreds of documents so far.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- Understanding scientific documents is an important step in downstream tasks such as knowledge graph building, text mining, and discipline classification.  [sources: Keyword Extraction in Scientific Documents]
- The results verify the hypothesis that trainable access to a similar (yet still different) page together with its already known target information improves the information extraction.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- The pipeline first structures and indexes documents through MLLM-based visual perception, then profiles and leverage the cross-page information distribution to construct reasoning-intensive QA pairs, as well as performs cascaded quality assurance operations via MLLM.  [sources: DocArena: Turning Raw Documents into Controllable Training Environments for Document Search Agents]
- The scientific publication output grows exponentially.  [sources: Keyword Extraction in Scientific Documents]
- Therefore, it is increasingly challenging to keep track of trends and changes.  [sources: Keyword Extraction in Scientific Documents]
- The automation of document processing is gaining recent attention due to the great potential to reduce manual work through improved methods and hardware.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- We will expand our previous work where we proved that convolutions, graph convolutions and self-attention can work together and exploit all the information present in a structured document.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- Taking the fully trainable method one step further, we will now design and examine various approaches to using siamese networks, concepts of similarity, one-shot learning and context/memory awareness.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- The aim is to improve micro F1 of per-word classification on the huge real-world document dataset.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- Furthermore, the experiments confirm that all proposed architecture parts are all required to beat the previous results.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
- The best model improves the previous state-of-the-art results by an 8.25 gain in F1 score.  [sources: Learning from similarity and information extraction from structured documents]
