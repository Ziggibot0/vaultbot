- The ancient and medieval roots of interpretation 🔗 Hermeneutics did not begin in the modern university.  [sources: Hermeneutics: The Art and Science of Interpretation]
- The hermeneutic circle describes the interdependent relationship between our pre-understanding and our interpretation of new information .  [sources: An Introduction to Hermeneutics: The Art of Interpretation]
- Modern hermeneutics has been broadly applied in the humanities, especially in law, history, and theology .  [sources: An Introduction to Hermeneutics: The Art of Interpretation]
- Biblical hermeneutics – the study of principles for interpreting scripture – was among the oldest systematic forms of interpretation.  [sources: An Introduction to Hermeneutics: The Art of Interpretation]
- For example, in theology, Biblical hermeneutics concerns the general principles for the proper interpretation of the Bible.  [sources: Hermeneutics - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy]

- Table of Contents What is hermeneutics?  [sources: An Introduction to Hermeneutics: The Art of Interpretation]

- In an important essay, “The Rise of Hermeneutics,” Dilthey affirms that the understanding achieved in the human sciences involves interpretation.  [sources: Hermeneutics - Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy]
